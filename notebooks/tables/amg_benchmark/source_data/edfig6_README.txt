Source Data Extended Data Figure 6

What it is. The values behind Extended Data Figure 6: the geNomad-viral share of calls and the Metabolism KO, AMG-weight and not-cautious shares of geNomad-viral calls at every tool tier (panel A), and the AMG-weight composition (panel B) and cautious-function composition (panel C) of the calls at every tier, one file per panel. All three files use the 12 tiers of Extended Data Figure 5C (CheckAMG (high), CheckAMG (medium), CheckAMG (low), DRAM-V (aux 1), DRAM-V (aux 2), DRAM-V (aux 3), DRAM-V (allow T), DRAM-V (aux 4), DRAM-V (aux 4, allow T), VIBRANT (high), VIBRANT (medium), VIBRANT (low)). Each call is counted at every tier it belongs to. CheckAMG and VIBRANT tiers are cumulative, as in Figure 3, so each tier includes the calls of the tiers above it. DRAM-V (aux 1), (aux 2) and (aux 3) hold the calls of the default DRAM-V configuration at auxiliary score 1, at most 2 and at most 3, as in Figure 3, so DRAM-V (aux 3) holds all 47,308 default calls. DRAM-V (allow T), (aux 4) and (aux 4, allow T) are full configurations that include the default calls, 52,012, 160,941 and 171,833 calls. In the panel A file every share is from 0 to 1, and in the panel B and C files pct is a share of n_tier, from 0 to 1.

Files:

1. edfig6A_tier_tradeoffs.csv (panel A). One row per tier, 12 rows. n is the calls of the tier, which sets point size, viral the geNomad-viral calls and viral_share their share of n, the x axis. The three y axes are shares of the viral calls: metabolism_among_viral (metabolism, the viral calls with a KEGG Metabolism KO under the primary rule, over viral), weight_ge_0.6_among_viral (AMG weight at least 0.6) and not_cautious_among_viral (not cautious, where the n_viral_unannotated viral calls with no annotation have no cautious assignment and are not counted as not cautious). level is the marker shape, high / aux 1, medium / aux 2, low / aux 3 or aux 4, and allow_T marks the DRAM-V configurations that keep T-flagged genes, drawn as open squares. Columns: set, n, viral, metabolism, n_viral_unannotated, weight_ge_0.6_among_viral, not_cautious_among_viral, tool, tier, level, allow_T, viral_share, metabolism_among_viral.
2. edfig6B_weight_composition_all_tiers.csv (panel B). One row per tier and AMG weight bin, 58 rows, with the bins 'AMG weight ≥ 0.8', 'AMG weight 0.6 to 0.8', 'AMG weight 0.4 to 0.6', 'AMG weight < 0.4', 'Annotated, no AMG weight' and 'No annotation'. n_tier is the number of calls of the tier, for example 6,728 for CheckAMG (medium), 170,161 for CheckAMG (low) and 160,941 for DRAM-V (aux 4). Columns: tool, tier, AMG weight bin, n, pct, n_tier.
3. edfig6C_cautious_composition_all_tiers.csv (panel C). One row per tier and cautious-function category, with not cautious split by AMG weight, 91 rows. Categories: Essential gene, Nucleotide metabolism, Glucan metabolism, Lipid metabolism, Methylation, Not cautious (weight ≥ 0.6), Not cautious (weight < 0.6) and Not cautious (no weight). n_tier is as in panel B. Columns: tool, tier, Cautious functions, n, pct, n_tier.
